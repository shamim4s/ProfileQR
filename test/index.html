Android vCard Test

1. Contact — No Picture

Tests a normal vCard without any photo.

Download / Open VCF

2. Contact — Picture URL

The VCF contains a PHOTO URL. Android may or may not download the remote image depending on the Contacts app.

Download / Open VCF

3. Contact — Embedded Base64 Picture

This is the most important test. The image is downloaded and embedded directly inside the VCF as Base64.

Download / Open Base64 VCF
Android note: Different Android phones use different Contacts applications. Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus, etc. may handle vCard photos differently. The Base64 version gives the Contacts app the actual image data instead of requiring it to download an external image.
