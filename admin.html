Online QR: the QR opens a public profile page. The generator uploads profile.json, contact.vcf, qr.png, and profile-url.txt into profiles/<profile-id>/ in your GitHub repository.
Offline QR: the QR contains the vCard itself, so the contact can still be saved without the profile website.
