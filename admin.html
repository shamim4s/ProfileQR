ProfileQR

Contact QR Generator

Create an offline contact QR or publish a complete online profile package to GitHub Pages.

Online QR: the QR opens a public profile page and publishes the profile package to GitHub.
Offline QR: the QR contains the contact vCard itself. The QR payload intentionally excludes the profile photo so it remains small enough to scan reliably.
